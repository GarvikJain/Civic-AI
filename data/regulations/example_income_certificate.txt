Section 2. Eligibility

An applicant is eligible for an income certificate under this example scheme if
the total annual household income is below 2,50,000 rupees, the applicant has
been a resident of the district for at least one year, and the applicant is not
already holding a valid income certificate issued in the same financial year.

Section 3. Required Documents

The applicant must submit proof of identity, proof of residence, and proof of
income. Proof of identity may be an Aadhaar card or a voter identity card.
Proof of residence may be a ration card or a registered rent agreement. Proof
of income may be a salary slip, an employer certificate, or a self-declaration
attested by a revenue officer.

Section 4. Processing Time

An application filed with complete documents is processed within fifteen
working days. If any document is missing, the applicant is informed within
three working days and the application is held for thirty days pending the
missing document.

Section 5. Appeals

An applicant whose application is rejected may appeal to the Sub-Divisional
Officer within sixty days of the rejection date. The appeal must state the
reason for rejection quoted in the rejection order.
